<h1 align="center">DirWiz</h1>

<p align="center">Every byte on your Mac, mapped. A fast disk space visualizer with a living treemap that updates itself as your files change.</p>

<p align="center"><a href="https://dirwiz.app">dirwiz.app</a></p>

---

This is the public home for DirWiz bug reports, feature requests, and release notes. The app itself is closed source and distributed through [dirwiz.app](https://dirwiz.app).

## Reporting a bug

Open an [issue](https://github.com/okturan/dirwiz/issues) with:

- your macOS version and Mac model
- the DirWiz version (DirWiz menu, About DirWiz)
- what you did, what you expected, what happened

For anything security related, email okanert@gmail.com instead of opening a public issue.
